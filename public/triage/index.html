How it works

Read, never invented.

Every figure, fee, threshold and rule in an answer comes from a source the line names under it — the regulator’s page, the statute, the platform’s own help centre, the publisher’s own report — with the year it was read. If the line does not know, it says so rather than guess.

The engine recommends; a person decides.

The line reads your question for where you stand and whether the specifics are yours — a figure on the table, a contract in hand, something gone wrong. When they are, it offers a person on the team, who reads the thread with the engine’s notes beside it and answers in their own words. A person is on the line Monday to Friday, 10 AM to 3 PM Pacific.

Not advice. Readiness.

Nothing on the line is legal, tax or financial advice — a lawyer licensed where you live and an accountant are the people for that. The line is good at making you ready for those conversations, and for the one with the brand.
