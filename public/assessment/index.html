      How it works

      Scored, not guessed.

      Every answer weighs on every service

      Each card you pick adds to some of the nine services and not others. The one with the most weight at the end is your fit; the one behind it is the runner-up. Ties go to the fixed-price tier — the smaller ask and the faster win.

      Both tiers are on the table

      Five of the services are fixed-price builds you can book today. Four are scoped in writing before any price exists. The result tells you which kind yours is, and the first step for each.

      A person still reads it

      The desk gets a copy of your answers with your fit. If you write to us afterwards, we start from what you told us here — not from a blank form. And if the answer is honestly none of the nine, we'll say so.

      Not sure it's a service at all?

      Creators looking for representation or community have their own doors — the front desk — and brands with a campaign in mind should work with us directly.
