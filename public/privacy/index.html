Privacy

The little we hold, held carefully.

This page covers the marketing site you're on now — createspacebrand.com. It's short because the site collects almost nothing, and we'd like to keep it that way.

In plain words

No analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising cookies, no third-party font or script servers. The one thing you can give us here is an enquiry — and we only use it to reply.

The brand enquiry form

If you send an enquiry from the For brands page, the details you type — name, work email, company, budget range, timing, and how you found us — are emailed to our partnerships team so a person can reply. That's the whole journey. Nothing is added to a marketing list, sold, or shared, and if we're not the right fit we say so and that's the end of it.

Keeping the form safe from abuse

To stop automated spam, the form applies a short-lived, per-connection rate limit. That involves briefly holding a connection identifier (an IP address) for up to an hour. It isn't linked to your enquiry or used for anything else.

Applications live elsewhere

Applying to Community or Talent happens in our workspace at createspacebrand.online, which has its own careful handling — including a standing rule that no protected attributes are ever collected or scored. This site only links you there.

Cookies and third parties

This site sets no cookies and loads nothing from third-party servers — even the fonts are served from our own domain. Season status shown on the doors is fetched from our own workspace, and tells us nothing about you.

Questions, or changes of mind

If you'd like an enquiry you sent deleted, or you have any question about the above, write to us through the contact form and a person will take care of it.

There's room for you here.

Pick your door at the front desk. Every application is read by a person, and every decision is made by one.
