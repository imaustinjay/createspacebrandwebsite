Brand Architect Jr.

Hold a house's standards, from the first stage.

The Brand Architect seat at its first stage — creative direction and the brand build on our campaigns, the Brand Architect Engine, and our own socials, under the founder's review. Fully remote, part-time, with a real path through the role.

Opens to applications Monday, November 2

What the seat holds

01

Ideate and Define

Creative direction and the brand build on every campaign: the concept, the brief, and the standards it is written against.

02

The Brand Architect Engine

The studio’s queue — briefs, the canvas, the library of approved assets, overlays and delivery — and the compliance read on every piece before it ships.

03

Our own socials

Brand Architect Socials: writing and packaging episodes in the house voice, and keeping the slate.

04

Paid engagements

The Brand Architect’s layers on the brand services clients hire us for, handed off in order.

Who it's for

Someone who has built a brand system another person could use, and kept it. One to three years of brand or identity work is typical; a strong case matters more than the count. If you're earlier than that, the Brand Architect Intern cohort is the way in — and its direct hire lands in this seat.
