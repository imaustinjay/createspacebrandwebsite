Content Creator · Contract

Make the createspace page, two pieces a week.

Short-form organic content for the createspace brand page, and only for it: pieces based on trends, specific content concepts and repeatable formats, most of them 7–10 seconds long. Each week we send you the assignments, with links to the content that inspired them — you make them exactly as outlined. A low-volume role, with two openings.

Applications open

What the role holds

01

Short-form, weekly

Every week you get two content assignments. They are short-form: trend-based pieces, specific content concepts and repeatable formats, and most run 7–10 seconds.

02

Made exactly as outlined

Each assignment comes with the concept and links to the content that inspired it: the hook, the beats, the on-screen text and the shots. You follow it and execute it as written — that is the job.

03

For our page only

Everything you make is organic content for the createspace brand page. Nothing for other brands, and nothing for your own feed.

04

UGC, when we offer it

From time to time we may offer you a UGC project for the brand, paid separately from your monthly assignments.

How we choose

01

Application read

We watch the posts you linked and read every answer.

02

A short conversation

Twenty minutes on video about how you work and what you make. This is where we go through the rate and the terms.

03

Your first month

A contract for your first month of assignments, two pieces a week.

04

Extending

Creators who do well are offered a longer contract with the brand.

Who it's for

A creator who already makes short-form organic content people watch, and who is happy to make someone else's idea well — quick, clean, and on time, every week. If you'd rather pitch your own ideas, or you're looking to join our talent roster, this isn't that door: creators joining the roster apply through casting.
