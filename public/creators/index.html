The application

Five steps, and a person at the end of them.

  1. Choose your door — Community or Talent.
  2. Work through the steps; it autosaves as you go.
  3. An open rubric scores everyone the same way — advisory only.
  4. A human reads the full breakdown and decides.
  5. You get a written letter, whichever way it goes.

Seasons are quarterly. The door tells you whether it's open before you start.
