What createspace does

Plainly, for anyone just arriving.

createspace · community + talent is a creator management company. We help content creators grow — together, in a community — and professionally, through talent representation. And we connect brands with our represented roster for managed influencer partnerships. Everything we offer is listed here.

A creator community

Membership for content creators at any size — no follower requirement. Cohort Studio sessions, peer support and steady creative practice, so growth doesn't have to happen alone.

Talent representation

Professional management for a represented roster of creators. We negotiate brand deals, handle the contracts and invoicing, and pay creators net within 24 hours — 72 at the absolute outside.

Brand partnerships

Brands brief our roster for influencer marketing campaigns and UGC. Proposals, negotiation, signing and invoicing run in one branded system, and our fee is billed to the brand as its own line — never deducted from the creator's gross.

The createspace brand app

createspace brand is our app — the workspace at createspacebrand.online where all of this runs. Creators apply to either division there, and represented talent use it day to day for live media kits, deal rooms and invoicing. It's the same createspace brand you'll see named when the app asks you to sign in.

Digital products & workshops

Creator planners, content systems and kits in the shop, plus live workshops and small cohorts — practical tools for creators building on their own.
